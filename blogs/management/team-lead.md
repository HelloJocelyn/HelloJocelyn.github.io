### Code of Ethics or Communication Guidelines
communicate in a constructive and respectful way to maintain a positive and productive atmosphere
1. Respectful Communication: Encourage language that’s neutral, solution-oriented, and empathetic. 

2. Collaboration Over Competition:Foster a collaborative atmosphere where team members are encouraged to work together and support each other.

We celebrate each other’s successes and help each other with challenges. Teamwork is the key to our success

3. Constructive Feedback:Ensure feedback is focused on the work, not the individual, and is aimed at improvement, not criticism
Provide feedback that is specific, actionable, and aimed at improvement. Use ‘I noticed’ or ‘I suggest’ instead of assigning blame.
4. Emotional Intelligence:Encourage members to be mindful of how their words or actions might affect others, especially during stressful situations.
5. Accountability:Team members should take responsibility for their actions and communicate openly when things go wrong, without resorting to blame.
Example: "Own up to mistakes and view them as learning opportunities. Focus on what we can do to improve moving forward."
